# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Couples planning a marriage proposal or a private celebration dinner in
Punta Cana. Most plan from abroad, weeks ahead, often on a phone, and
frequently in secret from their partner. They are booking a once-in-a-lifetime
moment with a local service they have never met, so their real question is
"can I trust these people with this?" The site is read in English, Spanish,
French and Portuguese.

## Product Purpose

The site sells private, fully coordinated experiences: beachfront proposal
setups (decor, photography, transportation) and private three-course dinners
on the beach in Cabeza de Toro. Visitors compare packages, personalize the
details and send a request for their preferred date; the team confirms
availability personally and a deposit secures the booking.

Success is a visitor who understands what is included, what it costs and how
the day runs, feels reassured enough to send a request, and arrives on the day
knowing exactly what to expect.

## Brand Personality

Romantic, calm, assured. Quiet luxury: the photographs carry the emotion and
the interface stays out of their way. The voice is warm and specific (what
happens, who meets you, when you pay), never breathless or salesy. The
existing black, ivory and gold identity stays.

## Anti-references

- **Resort excursion sellers.** Coupon-style tour booking: price badges,
  countdown timers, "only 2 left" pressure, stock photography.
- **Generic AI landing pages.** A small uppercase label above every section,
  identical card grids, numbered section markers, the same section layout
  repeated down the page.
- **Cold corporate booking software.** Dashboard-like forms and system
  language; this is a personal service run by people.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the moment.** Real photographs of real setups do the persuading;
   layout and type frame them rather than compete with them.
2. **Reassure at every decision.** Price, deposit, what is included and who
   meets you are always easy to find near the action that needs them.
3. **One clear next step.** Each section leads somewhere specific: proposals,
   dinners or a date request.
4. **Calm over clever.** Restrained motion, generous space and plain language.
   Nothing that would feel out of place at a candlelit table.
5. **Equal in every language.** Layouts hold up when Spanish, French or
   Portuguese copy runs longer than English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Best effort: readable body text contrast, visible
keyboard focus, keyboard-usable selectors and galleries, meaningful alt text
from the Studio, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
